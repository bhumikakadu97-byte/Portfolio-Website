Digital Fablab

Fusion 360🚀

Learn 3D CAD design, parametric modeling, and digital fabrication workflows.

Day 1

  • Laser cutting technology aur digital fabrication workflow ka intro.
  • Laser head, bed, aur control panel jaise components ki jankari.
  • Suitable materials: Acrylic, MDF, cardboard, aur plywood.
  • Safety rules aur machine operating precautions ki study.
  • Design software me basic vector drawings ki practice.

Day 2

  • Laser cutting ke liye 2D vector design ke basics.
  • Raster engraving aur vector cutting ke beech ka antar.
  • Lines, rectangles, circles, aur paths se shapes banana.
  • Stroke color, thickness, aur scaling ka dhyan rakhna.
  • Pehli laser-ready file prepare karna.

Day 3

  • Speed, power, aur frequency settings ko explore karna.
  • Different materials par laser energy ka reaction.
  • Optimal settings ke liye test cuts perform karna.
  • Kerf value aur tolerance adjustments ko samajhna.
  • Prototype cut karke design accuracy verify karna.

Day 4

  • Press-fit aur interlocking structures ke assembly concepts.
  • Material thickness aur joint clearance ke hisab se design.
  • Slot design, tab structures, aur modular techniques.
  • Easy scaling ke liye parametric design banana.
  • Parts ko cut karke functional structure me assemble karna.

Day 5

  • Raster aur vector engraving techniques.
  • Grayscale engraving concepts aur image preparation.
  • Power aur speed se engraving depth adjust karna.
  • Nameplates aur decorative panels jaise custom objects design karna.
  • Ek hi job me engraving aur cutting perform karna.

Day 6

  • Engineering applications aur mechanical prototyping.
  • Spur gear, brackets, aur frames jaise functional parts design karna.
  • Cutting ke baad measurement tools se accuracy check karna.
  • Material waste kam karne ke liye design optimization.
  • Real-world assembly ke liye laser-cut mechanical parts banana.

Day 7

  • Final laser cutting project ka end-to-end execution.
  • Design se fabrication aur assembly tak ka workflow plan karna.
  • Press-fit techniques se functional product fabricate karna.
  • Finishing quality, precision, aur structural stability par focus.
  • Project documentation aur portfolio submission.
